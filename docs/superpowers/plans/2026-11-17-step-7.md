## 2. Datensicherung: export and import

**Task:** save all entries of the workspace to one file, and bring them back into the same or another
browser or workspace.

- **Where:** a section "Datensicherung" on the organisation page, next to the organisation's details.
  It is a setting, not a catalogue tool.
- **Export:** one JSON file, `daily-<workspace>-<date>.json`, with format name and version, the day
  it was made, every entry (with its history) and the organisation's details. One click; nothing
  leaves the device in the preview.
- **Import:** pick a file; Daily reads it first and says what it will do: "37 Einträge, davon 35 neu,
  2 schon vorhanden". Entries keep their ids, so the links between them (VVT → DSFA → TOM, Vorgang →
  Beratungsvermerk) keep working. Entries already present are skipped, never overwritten. A file of
  an unknown format or a newer version is refused with the reason.
- **Store:** a `restore(records)` method on both stores (browser and API) that writes entries with
  their ids and history as they are; the API checks the workspace the same way it does for any write.
- **Reminder:** in the preview only, the section says when the last backup was made, from the
  browser's own memory of it. No banner, no nagging elsewhere.
- **Left out:** a new CSV or Excel export (every register list already exports CSV, and the DSFA
  exports to Excel); automatic or cloud backups (the server keeps its own); merging changed
  versions of the same entry.
- **Tests:** unit tests for the file format and the restore with ids and links; a preview e2e test
  that exports, clears the browser, imports and finds the linked entries again.

## 3. Datenschutzhinweise from a VVT entry

**Task:** hand people the information of Art. 13 DSGVO for one processing: applicants, customers, a
camera, a newsletter.

- **What:** a second print of a VVT entry, "Datenschutzhinweise nach Art. 13 DSGVO", under the letter
  head like the Auskunft. The register print (Art. 30) stays as it is.
- **Content, in the order of Art. 13:** controller and contact (para. 1 lit. a), DSB contact from the
  organisation details (lit. b), purposes and legal bases (lit. c), legitimate interests where Art. 6
  para. 1 lit. f is a basis (lit. d), recipients (lit. e), third countries and safeguards (lit. f),
  retention (para. 2 lit. a), the rights and the right to complain as fixed text (lit. b to d), and
  whether the data must be provided (lit. e) and automated decisions (lit. f).
- **What is missing in the VVT** stays a marked blank to fill in, as on the breach notice, never an
  invented sentence.
- **One new detail:** the controller's address, entered once on the organisation page (Art. 13 para. 1
  lit. a asks for contact details; letters can use it too). No new fields in the VVT.
- **Left out:** a website privacy policy generator with cookies and tracking (Website oder Shop starten
  and the Cookie- und Einwilligungs-Check cover the checks, and a website policy needs the site's own
  services); Art. 14 notices for data not collected from the person (rare; on demand); one notice
  combining several VVT entries (on demand).
- **Tests:** unit tests for the parts and the blanks; a print e2e test from the VVT example; legal
  review entry in `docs/legal-review.md`.

  workspace, archived ones and the organisation's details included, with its history as
  `daily-<workspace>-<date>.json`, and reads such a file back. Before writing, it says how many

  Datenschutzhinweise from a VVT entry.
- Step 2 is built: a VVT entry of a controller (not of a processor) has a second button,
  "Datenschutzhinweise drucken", next to "Drucken"; `?druck=hinweise` opens the entry with the notice
  on paper. The notice prints under a letter head (Verarbeitung, Für, Stand) in the order of Art. 13
  Abs. 1 and 2: controller with the new address from the organisation details, DSB contact, purposes,
  legal bases with their full citation, the interests where lit. f is a basis, recipients, third
  countries, storage period, the rights as fixed text with the right to object apart where lit. e or
  f is a basis and the withdrawal of consent where consent is, and the right to complain. The
  interests, the duty to provide the data and automated decisions get lines to fill in; empty VVT
  fields show "fehlt". No lines to sign. Any entry can have such a second document (`alsoPrints` in
  `prints.ts`). **Step 7 is done.**
