# Schulung: learning content for staff

Written 2026-11-16 against `main` at `cf24e94`. It plans the new catalogue area **Schulung** that
the owner asked for in the daily use review (section 3a of `2026-11-13-daily-use-review.md`). It is
meant to work without the conversation it came from.

## 1. Why

A data protection officer trains staff every year: new joiners, phishing, breaches, data subject
requests and, since Art. 4 KI-VO, AI literacy. Today Daily only records that a training took place
(Schulungsnachweis). The material itself comes from somewhere else: an old slide deck, a purchased
e-learning, a handout. The product roadmap (2026-11-10, section 3) lists "training content with
test and certificate" as the gap against the market.

Daily can close it in its own way: short modules whose every statement cites its article, carry
the Rechtsstand and print, presented by the DSB and recorded in the Schulungsnachweis afterwards.

Rule from the review: a tool must carry real daily work. Each module therefore ends in a step the
organisation needs anyway, a Schulungsnachweis entry, and points to the tool staff use afterwards
(for example the breach module to how a breach reaches the DSB).

## 2. Owner's decisions (16 Nov 2026)

| Question            | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Format              | Slides in Daily: a full-screen presentation in the browser and a printable handout. A PowerPoint export may come later.                                                                         |
| Who writes content  | Daily writes fixed modules and reviews them like the tools. The organisation fills slots for its own details: DSB contact, reporting channel, its own AI usage policy.                          |
| Test and attendance | A short quiz per module. Finishing a session pre-fills a Schulungsnachweis entry (topic, date, format, module and its version). No tracking per person: the quiz is answered together or alone. |

## 3. What a module is

A new tool shape `lesson` next to `questionnaire`, `register`, `workflow` and `utility`. A lesson
has no answers to store and no engine; it is content.

```ts
type Lesson = {
	slug: string; // e.g. 'schulung-datenpanne'
	version: number; // bumped when a statement changes
	audience: Localized; // "alle Beschäftigten", "Führungskräfte"
	minutes: number; // planned length
	topic: string; // a value of the Schulungsnachweis topics, e.g. 'pannen'
	slides: Slide[];
	quiz: QuizQuestion[]; // three to five
};

type Slide = {
	title: Localized;
	points: Localized[]; // at most five short lines
	example?: Localized; // a case from daily work
	notes?: Localized; // speaker notes, not shown to the audience
	cite?: LegalBasis[]; // every legal statement cites its article
	slot?: SlotId; // a slide that shows the organisation's own details
};

type QuizQuestion = {
	text: Localized;
	options: { text: Localized; correct: boolean; why: Localized }[];
	cite?: LegalBasis;
};
```

Slots are the only organisation-specific content: `dsb` (name and how to reach them), `meldeweg`
(how a breach reaches the DSB: e-mail, phone, form), `ki-richtlinie` (the clauses of the
organisation's own KI-Nutzungsrichtlinie, from its saved result). An empty slot shows a visible
placeholder in square brackets, as the policy print does, never invented text.

Dates follow the existing rules: AI Act dates from `DATES` and `since()`, no hand-written
"ab dem …" (`dates.spec.ts` will cover lessons too).

## 4. Screens

- **Catalogue**: a fifth area chip "Schulung" next to Datenschutz, KI, Audit, Entwickler, with its
  own icon. Lessons appear as tiles with audience and minutes.
- **Lesson page** `/werkzeuge/<slug>/`: what it covers, for whom, how long, its Rechtsstand, the
  slots it needs and whether they are filled, and three actions: Vortragen, Handout drucken,
  Schulung erfassen.
- **Presenter mode** `/werkzeuge/<slug>/vortrag`: one slide per screen, keyboard and swipe to move,
  speaker notes in a second window or below on the presenter's screen, the citation small at the
  foot of each slide. Ends with the quiz: question, answers, then the correct one with its reason.
- **Handout**: the print of the lesson, two slides per A4 page with their points and citations,
  then the quiz with answers and a line for the date. Uses the letter head (no Daily lines).
- **Schulung erfassen**: opens a new Schulungsnachweis entry with title, topic, today's date,
  format, the lesson and its version filled in. The DSB adds participants and evidence as today.

## 5. The SDM-Würfel

It moves to the Schulung area unchanged (category `schulung`), as the owner decided. It is a
method to learn and to teach a team, not a daily record. Its entries stay where they are. Later a
lesson "Das Standard-Datenschutzmodell in 20 Minuten" can use the cube as its exercise.

## 6. First modules

Ordered by how often a DSB needs them; each one names the tool it leads to.

| Module                                                   | Audience                        | Leads to                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Datenpannen erkennen und melden                          | all staff                       | the reporting channel; Datenpanne        |
| Datenschutz-Grundlagen für neue Kolleginnen und Kollegen | new joiners                     | Schulungsnachweis on joining             |

The AI module also completes the Schulungsnachweis' AI part (kind of involvement, systems used),
which is how Art. 4 literacy is shown.

## 7. Order of work

Each step is one PR with tests, screenshots and the legal review entry.

1. **Area Schulung**: the category, its chip, icon and filter; the SDM-Würfel moves there. Catalogue
   count unchanged. Small.
2. **Lesson shape and presenter**: the `Lesson` type, registry support, lesson page, presenter
   mode, handout print, page count cap in `print.preview.e2e.ts`. The first module, Datenpannen
   erkennen und melden, written with it, so the shape is tested on real content.
3. **Quiz and Schulung erfassen**: the quiz at the end of the presentation and in the handout; the
   pre-filled Schulungsnachweis entry (topic, date, lesson, version).
4. **Organisation slots**: where the DSB contact, reporting channel and the chosen AI policy are
   kept (today an organisation has only a name; in the preview the slots live in the browser like
   entries); the lesson page shows missing slots.
5. **Further modules**: Grundlagen, KI im Arbeitsalltag, Phishing, Anfragen Betroffener; one PR each.

## 8. Not in scope

- A learning management system: per-person accounts, invitations, reminders, certificates per
  person, SCORM. The organisation records attendance in the Schulungsnachweis.
- Video and voice-over.
- Editing the wording of a module. The organisation changes its slots, not Daily's statements.
- PowerPoint export (may follow once the presenter mode is in use).

## 9. Checks for every module

- Every legal statement has a `cite`. A new spec checks that each cited DSGVO, KI-VO, BDSG or
  TDDDG provision exists in the law data (the Normen index already parses citations; today no spec
  does this for tools either, so it can cover both).
- Each module appears in `docs/legal-review.md` and stays `beta` until reviewed.
- Rechtsstand shown on the lesson page and on the handout.
- At most five points per slide and fifteen slides per module (a spec enforces it), so a module
  stays within about 20 minutes.
- Print test: the handout has no controls, fits A4 and stays within its page cap.
